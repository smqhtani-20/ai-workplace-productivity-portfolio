# Responsible AI Use

## Accuracy
I compare every AI-generated name, date, number, and decision against the original source before it's used — as shown in the verification table in `05-verification/`. Missing information is labeled `[Not Specified]` or "Unconfirmed" rather than guessed.

## Accountability
I remain responsible for the final content sent under my name. AI drafts and organizes information; it does not own the technical judgment, the root-cause conclusion, or the final decision.

## Confidentiality
I do not enter real incident details, credentials, client data, or internal infrastructure specifics into unapproved AI tools. Every example in this portfolio (TaskFlow, the budget figures, the SSO migration) is fictional.

## Privacy
I remove or avoid personal identifiers unless necessary and permitted. No real colleague, client, or stakeholder information appears anywhere in this repository.

## Bias
I review AI-generated prioritization and recommendations (e.g., which risk to escalate first) for unsupported assumptions, and I ask for the underlying evidence before accepting a recommendation as-is.

## Human Oversight
A qualified person — me — reviews every technical communication, root-cause summary, and plan before it is sent or acted on. AI does not make the final technical, security, or people-related decision.

## Green / Amber / Red Classification

| Information Item | Classification | Reason | Required Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fictional TaskFlow launch scenario used across this portfolio | Green | Contains no real personal, confidential, or organizational information | Safe to use in an approved AI tool without restriction |
| Anonymized infrastructure/budget figures (e.g., "12% cut") used in prompts | Amber | Even without company names, real figures of this kind could reveal internal planning if they were genuine | Use only generic or fictional figures in any public or shared AI tool |
| Real production credentials, customer records, or incident logs | Red | Contains security-sensitive or personal data | Never entered into a general AI tool; handled only through authorized internal systems |

## Portfolio Safety Confirmation
- All examples are fictional or adapted from general course scenarios (TaskFlow, generic budget figures, a fictional SSO migration).
- No real client, colleague, or organizational records are included.
- No passwords, credentials, contracts, or security details are included.
- All AI-assisted content in this portfolio was reviewed and corrected by the trainee before inclusion.
